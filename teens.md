# Dreamforge — Teen anime fantasy (from 2026-10-01)

Audience: teens 13+. Every Short is NOT made for kids.
Format: one ongoing original series, told as numbered episodes (45–59 s each, vertical),
plus a "Lore" Short every 4th video (character profile, world map, magic system, creature guide).
Each episode ends on a hook/cliffhanger ("Next: …") so viewers come back.

## Series: SKYFORGE SAGA
World: the Shattered Skies — floating islands drifting above an endless cloud sea, linked by
chain-bridges and airships. Magic comes from "sky-shards", crystals that fell when the old
sky-kingdom broke apart. Mood: epic, mysterious, hopeful; dusk-orange and deep-teal palette,
glowing cyan shards, drifting embers.

Cast (original — keep designs consistent every episode):
- Ren Asaka (16) — main hero, messy black hair with one white streak, red scarf, dark-blue
  short coat, carries a cracked shard-blade that glows cyan. Stubborn, loyal, reckless.
- Lyra Vell (17) — airship pilot and inventor, silver bob haircut, goggles on her head,
  brown leather jacket with brass gears. Clever, sarcastic, brave.
- Kuro — small black fox spirit with three tails tipped in blue flame; speaks rarely, wise.
- The Hollow King — main antagonist, tall armored figure with an empty cracked crown and a
  violet glow where a face should be; wants to collect every sky-shard.
- Captain Mire — Hollow King's commander, scarred armor, double-bladed spear.

Content rules: fantasy action is fine (sword clashes, magic blasts, chases, energy beams,
dramatic falls) but no blood or gore, no deaths shown on screen, no sexualised designs or
romance beyond friendship, no profanity, no real-world weapons or real people.
Nothing that copies or resembles an existing anime, character, logo or soundtrack.
