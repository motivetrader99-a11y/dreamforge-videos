# CHIKU & GOLA — original cute-couple romance comedy (from 2026-10-14)

The channel's main series from 2026-10-18 (Skyforge Saga paused).
Audience: teens 13+, NOT made for kids. Tone: sweet, funny, relatable couple moments —
jealousy, silly fights, sulking, making up, big love. Every Short ends warm (or on a cute cliffhanger).

## Cast (original — keep designs consistent every episode)
- Chiku (she) — small cream-white bunny, one ear stands up and one flops over, a mint-green bow
  on the floppy ear, big sparkly brown eyes with two white highlights, coral-pink blush ovals.
  Dramatic, easily jealous, loves snacks and selfies. Pouts with puffed cheeks when upset.
- Gola (he) — round chubby orange-tabby kitten with three darker stripes on his head, a tiny
  sunflower-yellow neckerchief, sleepy half-lidded green eyes that go wide and shiny when he
  panics. Lazy, clumsy, always forgets things, would do anything to make Chiku smile.
- Side characters (use sparingly): Fizz (a show-off peacock-blue parrot who flirts with everyone
  and makes Chiku/Gola jealous), Nani (a wise old tortoise who gives advice).

Look: thick clean outlines, flat pastel cel colours, chibi proportions (head ≈ body), simple
cosy backgrounds (their little treehouse home, a park bench, a café, a rainy window, a night sky
with a big moon). Floating hearts, sparkles, anger "puff" clouds, sweat drops, sulky rain cloud.
Captions as big bubbly bold text (Poppins Bold). Optional tiny on-screen emoji-style reactions.

## Signature sound: the "tap-tap"
An ORIGINAL sound effect generated in code (never a recorded/trending clip): two quick bouncy
squeaky "boop-boop" plucks (rising pitch, ~0.12 s apart) + a soft marimba sparkle. It plays
whenever Chiku and Gola do their little happy "tap-tap" dance (bumping paws/foreheads), at
least once per episode, usually at the make-up moment. Music: original light ukulele/marimba-
style synth loop, with a sad slow piano-ish version for sulky moments.

## Content rules
- Wholesome only: hugs, hand(paw)-holding, forehead bumps, cheek boops, hearts. No kissing on
  the lips, nothing suggestive, no adult themes.
- Jealousy and fights are comedic and short; nobody is mean, controlling or cruel; every fight
  resolves with an apology or a sweet gesture.
- No profanity, no real people, no real brands.
- Nothing copying or resembling existing characters, sticker/meme couples or cartoons (no
  bear-and-panda couple or lookalikes), and no copyrighted or trending audio clips.
